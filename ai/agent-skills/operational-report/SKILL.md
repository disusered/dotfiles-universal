# Operational reports

State the outcome and any blocker that prevents completion. Identify the affected
artifacts with useful links, paths, versions, or issue keys. Distinguish local
changes from committed, pushed, merged, or published work.

Include meaningful validation and its limits. Explain findings or failures when
needed to assess the result; omit routine logs. Answer outstanding questions and
follow the requested format. A short result can be one paragraph.

For unfinished work, state what remains and who can act. If user input is required,
give the specific decision or verified action and explain why it is needed.
For a handoff, include the branch and worktree state, exact identifiers, and evidence
needed to resume. Reference existing plans and artifacts instead of repeating them.
